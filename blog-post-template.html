Why Economics Matters for Beauty Retention

[Replace this text with your blog post content]

Understanding market shifts, price sensitivity, and consumer trading behavior is the first step to building a brand that survives luxury pullbacks. When consumer wallets tighten, beauty is often the last category to be cut, but the way they buy shifts dramatically.

The Shift in Consumer Behavior

They might trade down from a $300 facial serum to a $150 option, or they might stretch the time between their regular purchases. If your retention strategy is blind to these macroeconomic factors, you will lose revenue.

  • Price Sensitivity: Are your customers waiting for sales?
  • Purchase Frequency: Has the gap between order 1 and order 2 widened?
  • Cart Composition: Are they buying bundles or single items?

Adapting Your Strategy

This is where 'Decode' comes into play. By analyzing your customer data through an economic lens, you can predict these behaviors and adjust your lifecycle messaging before the churn kicks in.
